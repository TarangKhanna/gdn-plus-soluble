Structural argument: each base component has a finite canonical input alphabet
and proved finite memory invariant. Payload lifting enlarges those alphabets
without altering the recurrent primitive maps. Finite controllers, sequential
copies, delay and padding introduce only the operations listed above. Induction
on the fixed finite composition therefore gives a finite set of rational
ordinary operands/products plus the explicitly retained integer-numerator maps.
Choose compatible D after this program and geometry, instantiate weights, then
choose p to bound parameters and all pre-round results and ordered partial sums.

No omitted scalar-operation class or new unproved arithmetic obligation was
found. This is a mathematical coverage argument, not a machine-checked universal
proof. It does not establish arbitrary grid changes, floating-point equivalence,
perturbation margins, optimal depth, efficient size or StateBench identification.
